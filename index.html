// THE STORY SO FAR
CH. 03

Hey, I'm Rizaan.

Hussain Rizaan on paper, Hussainburger everywhere else. I'm a freelance graphic designer, 3D artist and marketer from the Maldives, and I believe every brand, every product and every person has a story worth telling well.

I grew up surrounded by turquoise water and a whole lot of imagination. Comics, films and games taught me that a single frame can hold an entire world, and that's how I approach design: every poster is a panel, every brand is a character, every campaign is a chapter.

Design gives it a face. 3D gives it depth. Marketing gives it a voice. I like working across all three, because the best ideas don't care which software they were made in. They just need to make people feel something and remember it.

Right now I'm looking for a team where I can keep learning, take on real problems, and make work that people actually stop scrolling for.

“Good design is a story you don't have to explain.”
